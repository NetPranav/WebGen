### Phase 39 (Pen & pencil)

Draw vector paths on the canvas.

#### Added
- The Pencil (Shift+P) records a stroke with every coalesced pointer sample, its time and pressure, and on release fits it to a few bezier nodes.
- The Pen (P): click to place a node, drag to pull handles (Alt breaks the pair), click the first node to close. Enter or Escape finishes, Backspace removes the last node. A finished path is one undo step.
- Path layers (`svgPath`) keep their nodes (`svg.nodes`, with per-node time and pressure) and draw caps, joins and dashes; the stroke keeps its width when the layer is resized.
- `src/core/paths`, `pen.finish` and `pen.undoNode` commands.

#### Changed
- Escape no longer clears the selection while a pen path is being drawn.
